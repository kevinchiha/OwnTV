# 0.4-watch-status: design

## Goal

Within 15 minutes of a KevBox Live customer's TV failing with no good play since, Kevin sees it on
kevbox.dev and gets one Telegram message with advice, and he gets a second message when they are
watching again.

## Scope

- **OwnTV app (`kevbox/` layer, branch base `kevbox-live`)**: a play watcher that notices 30
  seconds of continuous live playback in the Live TV preview box (`LivePreviewEngine`) or full
  screen (`OwnTVPlayer`) and sends a play note through a new Supabase function
  (`SupabaseApi.recordLivePlay`, path `rest/v1/rpc/record_live_play`). It holds at most one unsent
  note and retries it like `usage/LiveCheckin.kt`. Release 5.1.0.15.
- **kevbox, database (branch base `main`)**: migration `supabase/migrations/0016_live_watch.sql`:
  three columns on `live_device`, `record_live_play_as` and its JWT wrapper `record_live_play`,
  and the table `live_watch_incident`. The functions are copied byte for byte into
  `packages/core/test/schema.sql`.
- **kevbox, check**: the incident rules and reads in `packages/core` next to `liveErrors.ts`; a
  route `POST /api/cron/watch-check` gated by `CRON_SECRET`; `deploy/kevbox-watch-check.service`
  and `.timer` (every 5 minutes); a Telegram sender in `src/lib/`; `TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN` and
  `TELEGRAM_CHAT_ID` added to `deploy/env.example` by name only.
- **kevbox, screens**: a watch status line per TV in the customer window's KevBox Live box
  (`src/components/kevbox-live-section.tsx`, fed by the existing
  `GET /api/members/[userId]/live-errors`), and a "Can't watch now" list at the top of the Live
  errors page (`src/components/admin/live-errors-view.tsx`, fed by a new operator-only
  `GET /api/live-errors/watch`).

## How it works

### Terms

- **TV**: one `(user_id, device_id)` pair in `live_device`.
- **Customer**: one KevBox login (`user_id`). Their client numbers are their
  `member_playlist.client_number` rows, sorted as `liveErrors.ts`'s `clientNumbersOf` sorts them.
  Their name is the Sheet1 `name` (read with `fetchSheet1Data()` from `src/lib/excel.ts`) of the
  first client number that has a Sheet1 row. With no client number, the customer is shown by email
  (`kevbox_auth_users.email`).
- **Play note**: one call to `record_live_play`. **Last play**: `live_device.last_play_at`.
- **Counting failure**: a `live_error` row whose `code` is any code in `live-error-codes.json` or
  any `http_NNN` code, except `guide_download_failed`, `guide_empty`, `guide_parse_failed`,
  `guide_unreachable`, `net_offline` and `behind_live_window`, and whose own `app_version` is
  5.1.0.15 or newer.
- **Blocking failure**: a counting failure whose code starts with `playlist_` or `account_`, or
  whose `account_status` is `expired`, `banned`, `disabled` or `wrong_login`.
- **Version compare**: split on dots, compare each part as a number. A missing version, or one
  with a part that is not a whole number, counts as older than 5.1.0.15. `99.99.99.99` (a local
  build) counts as newer.
- **Silenced customer**: every one of their client numbers gets a non-null answer from
  `vpsProxyBlock()` in `src/lib/vps-proxy.ts` (cancelled; missing from Sheet1, which counts as
  cancelled; or expired, meaning no `trexExpiryDate` or one whose UTC day is before today's). A
  customer with no client number is never silenced.

### The TV

A watcher reads the preview engine and the full-screen player. When a live channel has played
for 30 seconds without an error, a stop or a channel change, the TV builds a note: device id
(`DeviceId`), app version, the TV clock time as ISO-8601 with zone, and the proxy flag the error
reports already send (`settings.proxyConfig.usable`). Catch-up, VOD, multiview tiles and the Home
hero are not watched, as in `ErrorWatchers`.

The TV sends at most one note per 60 minutes. The exception: the first 30-second play after
`ErrorReporter` records any failure in this process goes out at once. A note that fails to send
stays pending; a newer note replaces it. The pending note goes again at the next 15-minute tick or
the next app foreground. Signed out, nothing is sent.

### The database

`live_device` gains `last_play_at timestamptz` (TV clock, parsed by `live_error_ts`),
`last_play_received_at timestamptz` (server time) and `last_play_proxy boolean`.
`record_live_play(p_device_id text, p_app_version text, p_played_at text, p_proxy boolean)` takes
the user from `auth.uid()` and calls `record_live_play_as`, which sets those three columns and
`app_version` on the caller's row, creating it when missing unless the member already has 20 TVs
(the check-in's cap). A `p_played_at` that `live_error_ts` rejects, or one more than a day ahead of
server time, becomes `now()`.

`live_watch_incident`: `id`, `user_id`, `device_id`, `started_at` (server time, see below),
`failure_last_at` (TV clock `last_at` of the failure that opened it), `code`, `http_status`,
`provider_host`, `proxy`, `advice`, `alerted_at`, `cleared_at`, `cleared_proxy`,
`cleared_alerted_at`. At most one open incident (`cleared_at is null`) per TV.

### When a TV opens an incident

Every 5 minutes the check looks at each TV whose `live_device.app_version` is 5.1.0.15 or newer.
It takes the TV's counting failures whose `last_at` is later than its last play (all of them when
it has none), ordered by `received_at`, and finds the qualifying row:

- the first blocking failure, or
- with no blocking failure, the row at which the running sum of `repeat_count` reaches 2.

The TV opens an incident when it has no open incident, a qualifying row exists, that row's
`received_at` is at least 15 minutes old and less than 24 hours old. `started_at` is that row's
`received_at`; `failure_last_at`, `code`, `http_status`, `provider_host` and `proxy` come from it.

A play note whose `last_play_at` is later than the incident's `failure_last_at` clears it:
`cleared_at` is set and `cleared_proxy` copies `last_play_proxy`. Nothing else clears an incident.
An open incident whose `started_at` is 7 days old or more turns grey; a later play still clears
it, but it never sends another message.

### Advice

Taken on each run from the newest counting failure (by `received_at`) after the last play; first
match wins. A change updates `advice` and `proxy` on the open incident and sends no message.

1. Code `account_expired`, `account_banned`, `account_disabled` or `account_wrong_login`, or
   `account_status` `expired`, `banned`, `disabled` or `wrong_login`: "Check the line on the IPTV
   panel (expired / banned / disabled / wrong login)."
2. `playlist_max_connections`: "Too many devices on this line."
3. `decoder_failed`, `audio_failed` or `drm_failed`: "Problem on the TV itself; the proxy won't
   help."
4. `proxy` is false or null: "Turn on the proxy. The TV picks it up within 15 minutes, or at once
   if they reopen the app."
5. Otherwise: "Proxy is on and it still fails: provider or line problem."

"Proxy" is what the TV reported with the failure (`live_error.proxy`), not the switch on
kevbox.dev. The TV reads its proxy at the playlist check every 15 minutes and on app foreground
(`KevBoxCoordinator`, `ManagedProxy`).

### Telegram

The check posts to `https://api.telegram.org/bot<TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN>/sendMessage` with
`chat_id`, `text` and `disable_notification`, as plain text. Times are Asia/Beirut, `HH:mm`, with
`dd/MM ` in front when not today in Beirut. The failure in words is `liveErrorTitle()` from
`src/lib/live-error-labels.ts`; a TV is named by `tvTag()`. The link is
`https://kevbox.dev/customers?client=<first client number>`, or
`https://kevbox.dev/members?member=<email>` without one. A customer label is
`<name> (client 23)`, `<name> (clients 23, 41)`, or the email.

Can't watch, one TV:

> 🔴 KevBox Live: Nohad (client 23) can't watch since 20:45. 513 · Channel list download failed.
> Turn on the proxy. The TV picks it up within 15 minutes, or at once if they reopen the app.
> https://kevbox.dev/customers?client=23

Can't watch, several TVs: the first line ends "can't watch since 20:45 on 2 TVs.", then one line
per TV, `<tvTag>: <failure>. <advice>`, then the link. `since` is the earliest `started_at`.

Watching again, sent when the customer's last open alerted incident clears:

> 🟢 KevBox Live: Nohad (client 23) is watching again, 20:58, proxy on.

The time is `last_play_received_at`; "proxy on" or "proxy off" follows `cleared_proxy`.

Provider down: when one run has 3 or more customers with unsent incidents on the same non-null
`provider_host`, all with `started_at` within 15 minutes of the earliest, it sends one message:

> 🔴 KevBox Live: 4 customers can't watch since 21:00, all on line.tellytv.pro. Most likely the
> provider is down; the proxy won't help.

followed by one customer label per line. Watching-again messages combine the same way: 3 or more
customers clearing in one run on the same `provider_host` get "🟢 KevBox Live: 4 customers on
line.tellytv.pro are watching again, 21:30." plus the labels.

A can't-watch message sent from 00:00 to 07:59 Asia/Beirut carries `disable_notification: true`.
Every watching-again message carries it.

### Which incidents send

- A can't-watch message covers a customer's incidents that have no `alerted_at`, only for a
  customer with no other open alerted incident, who is not silenced, and only while the incident
  is open, not grey, and its `started_at` is less than 24 hours old. Incidents that open while the
  customer already has an open alerted incident get `alerted_at` set with no message.
- A watching-again message goes out only for incidents that had a can't-watch message sent and
  were not grey when they cleared. Its success sets `cleared_alerted_at`.
- A failed send leaves `alerted_at` or `cleared_alerted_at` empty, so the next run tries again.
  With either Telegram variable unset, the check logs a warning, still opens and clears incidents,
  and still returns 200.

## Acceptance criteria

### TV app

- **AC-1** A live channel that plays 30 seconds without an error, stop or channel change, in the
  Live TV preview box or full screen, makes the TV call `record_live_play` with its device id, app
  version, TV clock time (ISO-8601 with zone) and `settings.proxyConfig.usable`.
- **AC-2** A channel that plays 29 seconds and then fails, stops or changes channel sends no note,
  and the 30-second count starts again from 0 on the next channel.
- **AC-3** With no failure recorded by `ErrorReporter` since the last sent note, a 30-second play
  59 minutes after that note sends nothing; one 60 minutes after sends.
- **AC-4** After `ErrorReporter` records a failure in this process, the next 30-second play sends
  its note at once, even 5 minutes after the last sent note.
- **AC-5** A note whose call fails (no network, an exception or a non-2xx answer) stays pending; a
  newer note replaces it, so at most one is pending; it is sent again at the next 15-minute tick
  or the next app foreground. A signed-out TV sends nothing.
- **AC-6** The note carries those four values only: no channel name, stream address, username or
  password.
- **AC-7** Catch-up, VOD, multiview tiles and the Home hero send no notes.
- **AC-8** The app adds no user-facing string. New files carry the fork-only header, changed
  upstream lines carry `// KevBox:` comments, and `tools/kevbox/check-divergence.sh` passes. The
  release is versionName 5.1.0.15.

### Database

- **AC-9** `0016_live_watch.sql` runs twice on the test schema without error. It adds
  `live_device.last_play_at`, `last_play_received_at` and `last_play_proxy`, creates
  `live_watch_incident` with a unique index allowing one open incident per TV, and its functions
  match `packages/core/test/schema.sql` byte for byte.
- **AC-10** `record_live_play` by a signed-in member sets the three play columns and
  `app_version` on their own row only; a second member's row stays unchanged. It creates a missing
  row, except when the member already has 20 TVs. An invalid `p_played_at` stores `now()`. Signed
  out, it writes nothing and raises no error.
- **AC-11** `authenticated` can execute `record_live_play` and `anon` cannot; neither can execute
  `record_live_play_as`. `authenticated` reads its own `live_device` rows and
  has no access to `live_watch_incident`. `kevbox_admin` has select, insert and update on
  `live_watch_incident`. No grant lets a member write either table.

### Check

- **AC-12** A TV whose `live_device.app_version` is 5.1.0.14, null or `5.1.0.beta` never opens an
  incident; 5.1.0.15 and 99.99.99.99 can. A failure row whose own `app_version` is below 5.1.0.15
  never counts.
- **AC-13** A TV whose only failures are `guide_*`, `net_offline` or `behind_live_window` never
  opens an incident, whatever their `repeat_count`.
- **AC-14** One `playlist_download_failed` row (http 513) with no later play opens an incident on
  the first run 15 minutes or more after its `received_at`, and not on a run at 14 minutes. The
  same holds for one `account_*` row, and for one row with `account_status` `expired`.
- **AC-15** One `stream_lost` row with `repeat_count` 1 never opens an incident. A second
  `stream_lost` row with no play between them opens one 15 minutes after the second row's
  `received_at`. One row whose `repeat_count` is 2 opens one 15 minutes after its `received_at`.
- **AC-16** A qualifying row whose `received_at` is 24 hours old or more never opens an incident.
- **AC-17** A play note with `last_play_at` later than the incident's `failure_last_at` sets
  `cleared_at` and copies `last_play_proxy` into `cleared_proxy`. A play note earlier than it
  clears nothing.
- **AC-18** While an incident is open, later runs open no second incident for that TV and send no
  second can't-watch message.
- **AC-19** The advice follows the five rules in order with the exact wording above. Unit tests
  cover each rule, Nohad's `playlist_download_failed` 513 with `proxy` false (rule 4), the same
  with `proxy` true (rule 5), and `proxy` null (rule 4). A newer failure with a different proxy
  flag changes the stored advice and sends no message.

### Messages

- **AC-20** A new incident sends one message in the "can't watch, one TV" format above, with the
  Beirut time, the label from `member_playlist.client_number` and Sheet1, `liveErrorTitle()`, the
  advice and the customer-window link. A customer with no client number shows the email and the
  `/members?member=` link.
- **AC-21** A customer with two TVs opening incidents in the same run gets one message naming both
  TVs. A TV of a customer who already has an open alerted incident opens an incident and sends
  nothing.
- **AC-22** When a customer's last open alerted incident clears, one watching-again message goes
  out with the play's Beirut time and "proxy on" or "proxy off". No watching-again message goes
  out for an incident that never had a can't-watch message sent, or that was grey when it cleared.
- **AC-23** Three customers with new incidents in one run on `line.tellytv.pro`, `started_at`
  within 15 minutes of each other, get one combined message; two customers get two messages; a
  null `provider_host` never combines. Three customers clearing in one run on the same host get
  one combined watching-again message.
- **AC-24** A can't-watch message sent at 07:59 Asia/Beirut carries `disable_notification: true`;
  one sent at 08:00 does not; one sent at 00:00 does. Every watching-again message carries it.
- **AC-25** A customer whose every client number is cancelled, missing from Sheet1, has no
  `trexExpiryDate`, or has one whose UTC day is before today's gets no message. A customer whose
  expiry date is today still gets one. A customer with one silenced and one active client number
  gets messages.
- **AC-26** An incident whose `started_at` is 7 days old or more shows grey on kevbox.dev, leaves
  the "Can't watch now" list, and sends no further message, even when a play clears it.
- **AC-27** When the Telegram call fails, `alerted_at` (or `cleared_alerted_at`) stays empty and
  the next run sends the message. With `TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN` or `TELEGRAM_CHAT_ID` unset, the check
  logs a warning, opens and clears incidents as usual, and returns 200. No can't-watch message
  goes out for an incident whose `started_at` is 24 hours old or more.
- **AC-28** `POST /api/cron/watch-check` returns 500 when `CRON_SECRET` is unset, 401 without
  `Authorization: Bearer <CRON_SECRET>`, and 200 with counts otherwise.
  `deploy/kevbox-watch-check.timer` runs it with `OnCalendar=*:0/5`, and the service has
  `OnFailure=kevbox-alert@%n.service`, like `kevbox-first-login-check`. `deploy/env.example`
  names both Telegram variables with no values.

### kevbox.dev

- **AC-29** The customer window's KevBox Live box shows one watch status line per TV, labelled
  with `tvTag()` when the member has more than one TV, first match wins:
  1. app below 5.1.0.15: grey "Old app (5.1.0.14): update to see watch status" ("Old app
     (unknown)" with no version);
  2. open incident and the customer silenced: grey "Cancelled", or "Subscription ended
     <date>" for this sheet row ("Subscription ended" with no date);
  3. open incident 7 days old or more: grey "No plays for 7 days; problem from <date> never
     cleared";
  4. open incident: red "Can't watch since <started_at>: <advice>";
  5. a last play: green "Watching OK, last good play <last_play_received_at>";
  6. grey "No plays reported yet".
  Times use the box's existing date-time format in the browser's time zone.
- **AC-30** A member with no TVs in `live_device` shows no watch status line; the box keeps its
  existing "Not seen on any TV yet" text.
- **AC-31** The Live errors page shows "Can't watch now" above its other sections: one row per
  open incident that is under 7 days old and whose customer is not silenced, newest `started_at`
  first, with customer, client numbers, TV (`tvTag()`), since when, the failure in words and the
  advice. A row opens `/customers?client=<first client number>`, or `/members?member=<email>`
  without one. With no such incident the list reads "Nobody has a watch problem right now."

## Non-goals

- Turning the proxy on automatically: Kevin wants to decide, and a proxy can't fix every failure.
- A "channel list loaded" note: a 30-second play already proves the list loaded.
- Holding messages back at night: they arrive silently instead, so nothing is lost by morning.
- Anything shown on the TV: the customer never sees watch status.
- KevBox TV: Live and TV data never mix.
- A dismiss button, an ignore list for Kevin's own or test accounts, and reminder pings: the grill
  rejected all three.
- Pruning `live_watch_incident`: at one row per problem it stays small; a later phase can add it.
- Channel names in play notes: "can they watch" doesn't need them.

## Decisions

- Grill: ran 2026-10-15, two rounds, ended with nothing open. Round 1: one playback failure alone
  does not count (channel-list and account failures do); "can't watch" pings are silent
  00:00-08:00 Beirut; one message per customer; provider-down batching at 3 customers on one
  provider server; grey "Old app" line below 5.1.0.15; the proxy advice says when the TV picks it
  up; problems never re-ping and turn grey after 7 days. Round 2: cancelled and expired customers
  never ping; no dismiss button; no ignore list for Kevin's own or test accounts; "watching again"
  is always silent.
- The server decides; the TV only reports. Rejected: the TV deciding and reporting "can't watch".
  A switched-off TV can't report, and a customer who gives up and turns it off matters most.
- Warnings go to kevbox.dev and Telegram. Rejected: kevbox.dev only. A page nobody has open warns nobody.
- "Can watch" means 30 seconds of live play without stopping. Rejected: channel list loaded. A list
  can load while every channel fails.
- A TV becomes "can't watch" 15 minutes after the failure. Rejected: at once. Most failures recover
  within minutes.
- Advice depends on the failure. Rejected: "try the proxy" every time. A proxy can't fix an
  expired line or a broken decoder.
- Ordering compares TV clock to TV clock (`last_at` against `last_play_at`); waits use server time
  (`received_at`). Rejected: TV clock for waits. TV clocks on these boxes are often hours off.
- auto-resolved: the 15-minute wait runs from the qualifying row's `received_at`. A merge moves a
  row's `received_at` to the merge time, and the server keeps no per-report time, so the wait can
  only run long, never short.
- auto-resolved: failures from a row whose own `app_version` is below 5.1.0.15 never count, so a TV
  updated minutes after an old failure is not flagged for it.
- auto-resolved: a TV with no `live_device` row or a non-numeric version counts as old.
- auto-resolved: cancelled and expired follow `vpsProxyBlock()` as it is, which compares UTC days,
  not Beirut days (the draft said Beirut). A client number missing from Sheet1 counts as
  cancelled there too. A customer is silenced only when all their client numbers are blocked; one
  with no client number is never silenced, so test accounts still ping.
- auto-resolved: signed out, `record_live_play` writes nothing and returns without error, like
  `record_live_errors`. Rejected: raising an error, which no existing wrapper does.
- auto-resolved: `record_live_play` keeps the check-in's 20-TV cap and leaves `last_seen` alone.
- auto-resolved: the check writes `live_watch_incident` directly as `kevbox_admin` (select, insert,
  update), the way kevbox.dev writes `member_playlist`. Members get no access to it. The draft's
  "nobody writes directly" holds for `live_device` only.
- auto-resolved: a pending play note retries on `LiveCheckin`'s 15-minute tick and on app
  foreground. The draft said "the next tick" with no interval.
- auto-resolved: a cleared incident's watching-again message retries like the can't-watch one,
  which needs `cleared_alerted_at`.
- auto-resolved: a new incident for a customer who already has an open alerted incident sends
  nothing; watching-again waits for their last open incident to clear.
- auto-resolved: a can't-watch message never goes out for an incident 24 hours old or more, so a
  long Telegram outage or a renewed customer doesn't flood old problems.
- auto-resolved: advice is recomputed every run from the newest counting failure, so the red line
  follows a proxy change without a new message.
- auto-resolved: a `live_error.proxy` of null counts as off (rule 4).
- auto-resolved: batching groups only incidents waiting in the same run; a customer already
  messaged alone is not re-sent in a batch.
- auto-resolved: the green line and the watching-again time show `last_play_received_at` (server
  time), not the TV clock.
- auto-resolved: the "Can't watch now" list leaves out silenced customers and grey incidents.
- auto-resolved: the status line sits in the KevBox Live box under the Proxy row. The TV list
  lives in `KevboxLiveErrors`, so the draft's "beside the proxy switch" means the same box.
- auto-resolved: the route is `/api/cron/watch-check` with `deploy/kevbox-watch-check.*`, named
  after `first-login-check`. It returns 200 when Telegram fails, so `OnFailure` pages only for a
  broken check.

## Open questions

## UI

- **Platform:** web, kevbox.dev only. The TV app gets no UI change (AC-8).
- **Look:** kevbox.dev's existing components and styles: the shadcn parts in
  `src/components/ui/`, the tables and text colours the KevBox Live box and the Live errors page
  already use (`text-destructive` for red, `text-muted-foreground` for grey). Nothing new.
- **21st:** no.

## Release order

1. Back up prod data, apply `0016` to prod as `postgres`, deploy kevbox.dev with the timer and the
   Telegram variables. This is harmless before any TV sends notes: TVs on 5.1.0.14 show "Old app"
   and nothing pings.
2. Release KevBox Live 5.1.0.15.
3. Kevin confirms on one TV: play a channel for 30 seconds, and the green line appears.

## Testing

- App: unit tests for the 30-second rule, the 60-minute limit, send-at-once after a failure, the
  pending note and retry, and no note for VOD or catch-up. The Gradle lock wrapper runs the suite.
- kevbox: SQL tests for `record_live_play` against `packages/core/test/schema.sql`; unit tests for
  opening and clearing incidents, the five advice rules, the 24-hour and version cut-offs,
  batching, silent hours, silenced customers and the Telegram retry; route tests for the cron
  secret; component tests for the status lines and the list.
- End to end on the emulator against a local Supabase: fail a channel twice, wait, see the
  incident; play 30 seconds, see it clear.
