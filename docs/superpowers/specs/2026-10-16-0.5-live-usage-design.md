# 0.5-live-usage: design

## Goal

On kevbox.dev Kevin sees, for each KevBox Live customer and across all of them, how often they
watch, how many honest hours they spend, and which channels, categories and programmes they
watch, for up to 13 months back, and he gets a list of paying customers who are drifting away or
have gone quiet.

## Scope

- **OwnTV app (`kevbox/` layer, branch base `kevbox-live`)**: a watching diary in
  `kevbox/usage/` built on 0.4's `PlayWatcher` engines and `PlaybackScene`. It cuts live play into
  stretches, stores them in a persisted queue, and sends them in batches through a new Supabase
  function (`SupabaseApi.recordLiveStretches`, path `rest/v1/rpc/record_live_stretches`). A
  remote-press hook: a `dispatchKeyEvent` override in `app/src/main/java/tv/own/owntv/MainActivity.kt`
  (one `// KevBox:` line; every `MainActivity*` launcher alias in `MainActivityColours.kt`
  subclasses it). Release 5.1.0.16.
- **kevbox, database (branch base `main`)**: migration `supabase/migrations/0017_live_usage.sql`:
  the tables `live_watch_stretch` and `live_watch_day`, the functions `record_live_stretches_as`,
  its JWT wrapper `record_live_stretches`, `rollup_live_watch_days` and `prune_live_usage`; the
  column `live_device.current_app_since`, which means re-creating 0015's `record_live_checkin_as`;
  the column `kevbox_live_settings.usage_size_checked_on`. Functions copied byte for byte into
  `packages/core/test/schema.sql`.
- **kevbox, jobs**: an hourly route `POST /api/cron/live-usage` gated by `CRON_SECRET` (the
  roll-up, then the once-a-day database size check), `deploy/kevbox-live-usage.service` and
  `.timer`, and the 13-month prune added to the existing `POST /api/cron/telemetry-prune`.
- **kevbox, rules**: usage reads, the counting rule's outputs, drift, gone quiet and the top lists
  in `packages/core/src/`, next to `liveErrors.ts` and `liveWatch.ts`.
- **kevbox, screens**: the customer window (`src/components/customer-detail-dialog.tsx`) gets four
  summary boxes and four tabs; a new Usage tab; a new KevBox Live page at `/kevbox-live` with
  Overview, Customers, Channels and Errors tabs, replacing the Live errors menu item
  (`src/lib/nav-items.ts`); `/live-errors` forwards to the Errors tab.

## How it works

### Terms

- **TV, customer, silenced customer, version compare**: as defined in the 0.4 spec
  (`2026-10-15-0.4-watch-status-design.md`, Terms). Customer name and client numbers are read the
  same way 0.4 reads them.
- **Current app**: `app_version` 5.1.0.16 or newer, by 0.4's version compare (`99.99.99.99`
  counts as newer; a missing or non-numeric version counts as older). The SQL in 0017 and
  `packages/core` (`versionAtLeast` in `liveWatch.ts`, with 5.1.0.16 as the minimum) apply the same
  rule.
- **Current app since** of a TV: `live_device.current_app_since`, the server time the TV first
  reported a current app, by check-in or by a stretch call.
- **Test TV**: a TV whose `live_device.app_version` is `99.99.99.99` (the emulator and any build
  from Kevin's PC).
- **Stretch**: one live channel playing without a break, on one surface (box or full screen), during
  one guide programme. Same play test as 0.4's `PlayRun`: live only, not catch-up, picture showing,
  no failure. Multiview tiles and the Home hero are never stretches.
- **Box stretch / full stretch**: `SceneSnapshot.inBox` true / false while the stretch played.
- **Programme** of a stretch: the title of the entry airing now on that channel in the stored
  guide, read the way `LiveViewModel.nowPlayingFor` reads it (`nowProgrammesFor`, with the TV's
  guide time offset, no extra calls to the provider). Blank when the stored guide has nothing airing
  on that channel.
- **App start**: a cold start or a return to the foreground (`MainActivity.onStart`).
- **Last press**: the latest remote key press, or the app start if later, at or before a moment.
- **Counted seconds** of a stretch: zero for a box stretch shorter than 120 s; otherwise
  `max(0, min(ended_at, last_press_at + 4 h) - started_at)`.
- **Left-on seconds** of a stretch: zero for a box stretch shorter than 120 s; otherwise its
  duration minus its counted seconds.
- **Browsing seconds**: the whole duration of a box stretch shorter than 120 s.
- **Hours**: counted seconds / 3600 everywhere on screen. Left-on and browsing never add to hours.
- **Beirut day / hour**: `Asia/Beirut`, summer time included. Every day, week (Monday to Sunday),
  month and hour-of-day on screen and in `live_watch_day` uses it.
- **Last 30 days**: the 30 Beirut days ending yesterday. **The 30 before**: the 30 days before
  that. **Last 7 days**: the 7 Beirut days ending yesterday; **the 7 before** likewise. **Last 4
  weeks**: the 28 Beirut days ending yesterday. **Today**: the Beirut day now.
- **Last 12 weeks**: the current Beirut week so far and the 11 weeks before it. **13 months**: the
  current Beirut month so far and the 12 months before it (at most 396 days, inside the prune
  window).
- **Server day**: the UTC day, as 0.3's daily cap in `record_live_errors_as` counts it.
- **Watched** in a period: at least one counted second in it.
- **Seen** in a period: `live_device.last_seen` (the check-in sets it) falls in it.
- **Last counted play** of a customer or TV: the end of the counted part of its newest stretch
  with counted seconds above 0. "N days" since it: today minus its Beirut day.
- **Diary start** of a customer: their earliest stored stretch.
- **Drifting**: a customer, not silenced, with a current-app TV, whose diary start is at least 60
  days before today, whose last-30-days hours are at least 40% below the 30 before, and at least 10
  hours below.
- **Gone quiet**: a customer, not silenced, with a TV whose current app since is at least 7 days
  ago, and no counted seconds in the last 7 days (including never).
- **Problem count**: the customer's `live_watch_incident` rows whose `started_at` falls in the last
  30 days.

### The TV

A diary watcher reads the same engines and scene as `PlayWatcher`. A stretch starts when the play
test passes and ends on a channel change, stop, failure, catch-up, the app going to the background,
a move between box and full screen, or the guide programme on that channel ending. The programme is
the title the guide shows as on now for that channel when the stretch starts (blank without guide
data). Stretches under 10 s are dropped.

Each stretch gets an id made on the TV (a random UUID), and carries: channel and category (the
scene's, as 0.3's error entries carry them), programme, `started_at`, `ended_at`, `in_box`,
`last_press_at`, provider host (the stream address's host only, through 0.3's `Scrubber.host`),
proxy flag (0.4's corrected source, `ProxyInUse`), app version and device id (`DeviceId`). No
stream address, username or password. Times are TV-clock ISO-8601 with zone.

Remote presses reach the diary through `MainActivity.dispatchKeyEvent` (key down only), which
Android calls for every key event the activity's own window gets. Home never reaches an app, so
leaving with Home is not a press; `onUserInteraction` is not used because Android also calls it on
Home, which would stamp every background close as a press. A press inside a pop-up that opens its own window
(`OwnTVPopup`, a Compose `Dialog`) does not reach it; the next press in the main window does.

Finished stretches go into a queue saved on the TV, tagged with the signed-in member. The open
stretch is saved every 60 s; after the app is killed, the next start finishes it at its last save.
Every 15 minutes, and when the app goes to the background, the TV sends up to 200 queued stretches
with its clock reading (`tv_now`). A 2xx answer removes them; anything else keeps them for the next
try. The queue holds 2,000 stretches and drops the oldest first. A member switch drops the other
member's stretches.

### The database

`live_watch_stretch` holds one row per stretch: user, device, TV stretch id, `started_at`,
`ended_at`, `in_box`, `last_press_at` (all shifted to server time), channel, category, programme,
provider host, proxy, app version, `received_at`, and `rolled_up_at` (null until the roll-up has
counted it). `live_watch_day` holds one row per Beirut day, user, device, channel and category:
counted, left-on and browsing seconds, and a stretch count. A stretch that crosses Beirut midnight
splits its counted, left-on and browsing seconds at midnight.

`record_live_stretches` takes the device id, app version, `tv_now` and the entries. It shifts every
entry by `now() - tv_now`, then stores it as `record_live_errors_as` stores errors: allow-listed
keys, capped lengths, the same scrub, and a daily cap. Times must be ISO-8601 with a zone, as
`live_error_ts` accepts them, but without its "before 2026 becomes now()" step: the shift fixes a
TV clock that is years off. An entry with an unreadable time, or a TV stretch id that is not 1 to
64 letters, digits or dashes, is dropped. A repeat of a stored TV stretch id changes nothing: the
first copy stays. Like 0.4's `record_live_play_as`, the call gives a new TV a `live_device` row
(`first_seen` = `last_seen` = now), updates a known TV's `app_version` and leaves its `last_seen`
to the check-in. It returns the number of entries stored. With no user, a blank device id, a new
TV past the member's 20, entries that are not an array, or an unreadable `tv_now`, it writes
nothing and returns 0 without an error.

The roll-up takes the stretches whose `rolled_up_at` is null, recounts from all stored stretches
every Beirut day they touch for that user and device, then sets `rolled_up_at` on exactly the
stretches it took. It can run any number of times. `kevbox_live_settings.usage_size_checked_on`
holds the Beirut day of the last size check. `prune_live_usage` deletes old stretches and day
rows, as `prune_live_errors` does for errors.

### Screens

Customer window: four summary boxes, then tabs Overview (contact, subscription, credentials),
KevBox Live (today's box, unchanged), Usage (nine blocks), Payments. KevBox Live page: Overview
(totals, can't watch now, hours per day, peak times), Customers (drifting, gone quiet, everyone
ranked, all TVs), Channels (channels, categories, programmes), Errors (today's Live errors page
without "Can't watch now" and the usage list).

## Acceptance criteria

### TV app

- **AC-1** A live channel playing full screen from 20:00:00 to 20:30:00, with no failure, ends as
  one stretch with `in_box` false, `started_at` 20:00:00 and `ended_at` 20:30:00 (TV clock).
- **AC-2** A channel change, a stop, a failure, catch-up starting, the app going to the background,
  a box/full-screen switch, the guide programme ending, and a guide title appearing for a stretch
  that started with a blank programme each end the open stretch at that moment; where play goes
  on, the next stretch starts at that same moment, with the new title. Each case has its own test.
- **AC-3** A stretch shorter than 10 s is never queued. Catch-up, VOD, Multiview tiles and the
  Home hero never make stretches.
- **AC-4** A stretch carries the guide's on-now programme title for its channel at its start, and
  a blank programme when the channel has no guide data. It never carries a stream address,
  username or password; a test feeds a scene whose stream address holds a login and finds neither in
  the queued entry.
- **AC-5** Every remote key press that reaches the main window updates the last-press time (via
  `MainActivity.dispatchKeyEvent`, key down), the app start (cold start or return to the
  foreground) counts as a press, and each stretch carries the last press at or before its end. A
  stretch closed by pressing Home carries the press before Home, not the Home moment. Presses inside an
  `OwnTVPopup` dialog window are not required to count.
- **AC-6** With the app killed 25 minutes into a stretch, the next start queues that stretch ending
  at its last 60-second save (24 or 25 minutes in), not at the next start.
- **AC-7** Queued stretches go out every 15 minutes and when the app goes to the background, at
  most 200 per call, with `tv_now`. A 2xx answer removes exactly the sent ones; a failed call
  (no network, exception, non-2xx) keeps them all.
- **AC-8** The queue survives an app restart, holds at most 2,000 stretches and drops the oldest
  first. When a different member signs in, stretches queued for the previous member are dropped,
  never sent with the new token. A signed-out TV queues nothing.
- **AC-9** The app adds no user-facing string. New files carry the fork-only header, changed
  upstream lines carry `// KevBox:` comments, and `tools/kevbox/check-divergence.sh` passes. The
  release is versionName 5.1.0.16.

### Database

- **AC-10** `0017_live_usage.sql` runs twice on the test schema without error, creates
  `live_watch_stretch` and `live_watch_day` with the columns above, and its functions match
  `packages/core/test/schema.sql` byte for byte. It ends with a ROLLBACK comment, as 0016 does: a
  no-op `record_live_stretches` with the live signature, which answers success and stores nothing,
  so TVs clear their queues; re-running the file restores it. A test applies the no-op and finds a
  call stores no row and raises no error.
- **AC-11** `authenticated` can execute `record_live_stretches`; `anon` cannot. Neither can execute
  `record_live_stretches_as`, `rollup_live_watch_days` or `prune_live_usage`, and neither can read
  or write either table. `kevbox_admin` can select both tables and execute the roll-up and the
  prune, and nothing more on the two tables. Called with no user, the wrapper writes nothing and
  raises no error.
- **AC-12** The same TV stretch id from the same user and device is stored once, however many
  batches carry it; a later copy changes nothing in the stored row.
- **AC-13** With `tv_now` 3 hours behind the server, every stored time is the TV's time plus 3
  hours; a TV clock 5 years behind is shifted the same way. After the shift, an entry starting more
  than 5 minutes in the future is dropped, one with `ended_at` before `started_at` is dropped, and
  one longer than 24 hours is cut to 24 hours. An entry with an unreadable time is dropped; a call
  with an unreadable `tv_now` stores nothing and raises no error.
- **AC-14** Channel and category are capped at 120 characters and programme at 160, after the 0.3
  scrub (URL-shaped text to `[url]`, `username=`/`password=` values and
  `/live|movie|series/<u>/<p>/` paths to `[redacted]`). One call stores at most 200 entries. One
  member stores at most 5,000 new stretches per server day; past that, entries are skipped. A new
  device id past the member's 20 TVs is ignored, as 0.4 does.
- **AC-15** Deleting the auth user deletes their stretches and day rows.
- **AC-42** `live_device.current_app_since` is set to `now()` the first time
  `record_live_checkin_as` or `record_live_stretches_as` sees that TV report a current app, and
  never changes after. It stays null while the TV reports an older version, and a later downgrade
  leaves it set. The re-created `record_live_checkin_as` keeps 0015's behaviour otherwise; 0015's
  check-in tests still pass.

### Counting and jobs

- **AC-16** The roll-up gives, for a fixture of known stretches: a full stretch of 1 h counts 3,600
  s; a box stretch of 119 s counts 0 and adds 119 browsing seconds; a box stretch of 121 s counts
  121; a 6-hour full stretch whose last press is at its start counts 4 h and adds 2 h left-on.
- **AC-17** A stretch from 23:30 to 00:30 Beirut puts 30 minutes in each Beirut day; the fixture
  includes a day when Lebanon's clocks change.
- **AC-18** Stretches received today for a day 9 days ago change that day's rows on the next run;
  running the roll-up twice in a row changes nothing the second time.
- **AC-19** `POST /api/cron/live-usage` answers 401 without the `CRON_SECRET` bearer, and with it
  runs the roll-up and then the size check. `deploy/kevbox-live-usage.timer` runs it every hour.
- **AC-20** The size check runs on the first run of each Beirut day at or after 03:00 and on no
  other run: it runs when the Beirut time is 03:00 or later and
  `kevbox_live_settings.usage_size_checked_on` is not today, and sets it to today before sending.
  It sends one Telegram message that day when `pg_database_size` is over 4 GB, naming the size, and
  nothing at or under it. A Telegram failure is not retried that day and does not fail the roll-up.
- **AC-21** `telemetry-prune` also calls `prune_live_usage`, which deletes stretches whose
  `started_at`, and day rows whose day, is more than 396 days old; younger rows stay. It runs after
  the existing prunes, and a failure in it does not stop them.

### Rules

- **AC-22** Drifting, for fixtures: 50 h then 28 h is drifting (44% and 22 h down); 20 h then 13 h
  is not (35%); 20 h then 11 h is not (9 h); a customer with diary start 59 days ago is not; a
  silenced customer is not; a customer whose only TV is on 5.1.0.15 is not.
- **AC-23** Gone quiet, for fixtures: a TV with current app since 10 days ago and no stretches is
  listed as "Never watched yet"; one whose last counted play was 8 days ago is listed with "No plays
  for 8 days"; 6 days ago is not listed; a TV first seen 200 days ago with current app since 3 days
  ago and no stretches is not listed; a silenced customer is not listed.
- **AC-24** Hours, top channels, top categories and top programmes count only counted seconds.
  Programmes group by exact title and channel ("News" on two channels is two rows); blank titles are
  left out of programme lists.
- **AC-25** Usage belongs to the login: two client numbers of one login show the same numbers, and
  two TVs of one login add up.

### Customer window

- **AC-26** The window shows four summary boxes above the tabs: Can watch (0.4's status line,
  short form), Last 30 days (hours and percent change from the 30 before, or "new" in place of the
  percent when the 30 before had no hours), Trex expiry, Payment. Without a KevBox Live login the
  first two read "Not on KevBox Live". The short form is 0.4's line up to its first colon,
  semicolon or comma followed by a space, in its colour ("Can't watch since <time>", "Watching OK", "Old app
  (5.1.0.14)"). With several TVs it shows a red line if any TV has one, else a green line if any
  has one, else the first TV's line in the KevBox Live box's order; with no TV, 0.4's "Not seen on
  any TV yet". When both periods have no hours, Last 30 days reads "0 h" with no percent.
- **AC-27** Tabs Overview, KevBox Live, Usage, Payments hold, in that order: contact,
  subscription, credentials; today's KevBox Live section unchanged; the Usage blocks; payment
  history. Every control in today's window is still there.
- **AC-28** `/customers?client=241&tab=usage` opens client 241's window on the Usage tab
  (`tab=overview|live|usage|payments`); a missing or unknown `tab` opens Overview. The Usage tab
  fetches nothing until it is first opened. Customer links on the Errors tab and in 0.4's watch
  Telegram messages (`src/lib/watch-customers.ts`) carry `tab=live`; search, the command palette
  and the customer table keep opening Overview.
- **AC-29** The Usage tab shows, top to bottom: a drift warning ("Watching N% less than usual: X h
  in the last 30 days, Y h in the 30 before", plus the problem count when above 0) only when the
  customer is drifting; hours per week for the last 12 weeks with a 13 months (per month)
  switch; one square per day for the last 30 days with "Watched on N of the last 30 days", the
  average hours per watching day and the last counted play.
- **AC-30** Then: top 10 channels and top 10 categories (share of hours), each with a 7 days / 30
  days / 13 months switch defaulting to 30 days; top 10 programmes of the last 30 days with channel
  and hours, with no switch; a weekday by hour grid of counted minutes over the last 30 days.
- **AC-31** Then the diary: stretches newest first, 50 per page, back 13 months, filterable by
  channel and date range; each row shows start, channel, programme, duration; a stretch with
  left-on seconds shows "left on?". Box stretches under 2 minutes are hidden until a "Show
  browsing" switch above the diary is on; then they show, marked "browsing".
- **AC-32** With more than one TV, a per-TV block shows each TV's hours in the last 30 days under
  its `tvTag()` label (`src/lib/live-error-labels.ts`), and a TV filter narrows every Usage block to
  one TV. With one TV, neither shows.
- **AC-33** A customer whose TVs are all below 5.1.0.16 sees "Old app: update to see usage" in the
  Usage tab instead of the blocks.

### KevBox Live page

- **AC-34** The menu and the phone tab bar show "KevBox Live" at `/kevbox-live` in place of "Live
  errors" (the `NAV_ITEMS` entry's `title` and `tabLabel`), and the page header
  (`src/components/header-content.tsx`) reads "KevBox Live" there.
  `?tab=overview|customers|channels|errors` opens that tab (Overview otherwise), and `/live-errors`
  forwards to `/kevbox-live?tab=errors`.
- **AC-35** Overview shows: TVs that watched today, in the last 7 days and in the last 30 days, each
  "N of M" where M is TVs seen today or in the last 30 days; "Recording on N of M TVs", N being
  those of the M on a current app; hours in the last 7 days with percent change from the 7 before
  (with AC-26's "new" and "0 h" rules); the can't-watch count; the drifting count. Clicking a count
  opens its list: the TV counts and "Recording on" open Customers at All TVs, the can't-watch count
  scrolls to "Can't watch now", the drifting count opens Customers at Drifting. The hours figure is
  not a link.
- **AC-36** Overview then shows 0.4's "Can't watch now" list, unchanged except that test TVs are left out (AC-43), then hours per Beirut day
  for the last 30 days with a 13 months (per month) switch, then a weekday by hour grid of how many
  TVs had counted play in each hour, averaged over the last 4 weeks.
- **AC-37** Customers shows Drifting (name, last 30 days against the 30 before, percent, problem
  count, Trex expiry; soonest expiry first, none last), Gone quiet ("Never watched yet" rows first,
  then most days since the last counted play first), and every customer with a KevBox Live TV
  ranked by hours with a 7 days / 30 days / 13 months switch, each with their top category for the
  same period (zero-hour customers last). Last comes "All TVs": the "KevBox Live usage" list from
  today's Live errors page, moved here unchanged except for its customer links (AC-40) and with test TVs left out (AC-43).
- **AC-38** Channels shows top channels, top categories and top programmes, up to 20 rows each,
  each with hours and the number of customers who watched, a 7 days / 30 days / 13 months switch
  defaulting to 30 days, and a sort by hours (the default) or by customers.
- **AC-39** Errors shows today's Live errors page without its "Can't watch now" and "KevBox Live
  usage" sections, and with test TVs left out of its counts, chart and rows (AC-43); its existing
  tests still pass after the move.
- **AC-40** Every customer name on the Overview, Customers and Channels tabs links to
  `/customers?client=<first client number>&tab=usage`; a customer with no client number shows the
  login email and no link. Two lists keep AC-28's `tab=live`: 0.4's "Can't watch now" list (its
  links come from `src/lib/watch-customers.ts`, and a customer with no client number keeps 0.4's
  `/members?member=` link) and the Errors tab.
- **AC-41** Usage routes answer only an operator session (401 otherwise), through
  `requireOperator` like 0.4's `/api/live-errors/watch`.
- **AC-43** Test TVs are left out of every total, count, list and chart on `/kevbox-live`; a
  fixture with one test TV watching 10 h changes none of them. The test TV's own login still shows
  its usage in the customer window.

## Non-goals

- Picture quality (start-up time, freezing, resolution): not one of Kevin's goals (drift, what
  matters); the diary can take it later.
- Account sharing checks: not one of his goals.
- Rising and falling channels, and hours per provider server: too few TVs (9) and one main provider
  for them to say anything yet; the diary stores what both need.
- A weekly Telegram digest of drifting customers: later; the Customers tab lists them for now.
- Anything shown on the TV, including a notice that viewing is recorded: the phase adds no
  user-facing string to the app (AC-9).
- Naming TVs: they show by model through `tvTag`.
- KevBox TV: its Fleet page and tables are untouched; Live and TV data never share a table.
- Fuzzy matching of programme titles: programmes group by exact title and channel (AC-24).

## Decisions

- Purpose: spot drifting customers and learn which channels, categories and programmes matter.
  Rejected: picture quality, sharing. Kevin's pick.
- Hours: full screen counts; a box stretch counts once it passes 2 minutes; after 4 hours with no
  press the rest is left-on, kept out of hours. Rejected: everything counts (a forgotten box tops
  the charts), full screen only (misses box viewers). Netflix asks after about 90 minutes; live TV
  runs longer untouched, so 4 hours.
- Keep stretches and day rows 13 months. Rejected: 90 days of stretches (KevBox TV's rule), totals
  forever. Why: programme-level year-on-year (Ramadan). Supabase is on Pro (8 GB included; 67 MB
  used on 2026-10-16), so it fits.
- One phase, one release. Rejected: shipping the TV diary first so history piles up sooner.
  Kevin's pick.
- Customer window: tabs with summary boxes. Rejected: a separate full page (one more click to the
  proxy switch), two folding columns (a long list on the phone).
- Usage tab keeps all nine blocks.
- KevBox Live page with tabs replaces Live errors. Rejected: a separate Live usage menu item (menu
  grows), a switch inside Fleet (films and episodes against channels and programmes hardly match).
- Drift compares the last 30 days with the 30 before, at 40% and 10 hours, after 60 days of
  diary. Rejected: last 7 days against the 4 weeks before (Kevin preferred steadier), half of usual
  without guards (light watchers flood it).
- Each drifting customer shows a problem count: drift caused by failures is fixable.
- Approach: the TV keeps the diary and sends finished stretches in batches. Rejected: hourly
  play notes carrying channel and minutes (no diary, rough hours); a per-minute heartbeat (about
  430,000 calls a day at 300 TVs, gaps when offline); an outside analytics service (family viewing
  data with a third party, awkward join with Sheet1); reading the provider panel (unverified, rough).
- The counting rule runs on the server from raw stretches, so changing 2 minutes or 4 hours later
  recounts 13 months without an app update.
- Batch clock shift by `tv_now`. Rejected: trusting TV clocks (0.4 found them hours off).
- The roll-up recounts any day that gained stretches. Rejected: redo the last two days only (misses
  a TV offline for a week).
- Grill round 1: gone quiet starts its 7 days at `current_app_since`, stored per TV. Rejected:
  `first_seen` (months old for an updated TV, so "Never watched yet" on update day), the diary
  start alone (misses TVs that never watch).
- Grill round 1: the roll-up runs every hour, the size check once a day. Rejected: nightly at
  03:30 (numbers up to a day old). Recounting is cheap at this size.
- Grill round 1: browsing rows hidden in the diary behind a "Show browsing" switch. Rejected:
  always shown (guide scrolling buries real viewing).
- Grill round 1: test TVs (99.99.99.99) left out of the KevBox Live page, kept in their own
  Usage tab. Rejected: counting them (with 9 TVs, testing skews every list). Kevin's own real TV
