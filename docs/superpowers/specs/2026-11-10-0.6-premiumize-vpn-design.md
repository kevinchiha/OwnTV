# 0.6-premiumize-vpn: design

## Goal

Kevin can switch on a Premiumize VPN for one KevBox Live customer in kevbox.dev and pick its
server. That customer's TV then sends KevBox Live's traffic through an encrypted connection that
their internet company cannot inspect. If the VPN keeps failing, the TV silently goes back to
normal internet, Kevin gets one Telegram message, and the TV tries the VPN again 3 hours later.

## Scope

- **OwnTV app (`kevbox/` layer, branch base `kevbox-live`)**: the OpenVPN for Android engine
  (github.com/schwabe/ics-openvpn) built into the app. A new `kevbox/vpn/` package:
  `ManagedVpn` (the VPN twin of `network/ManagedProxy.kt`), a controller that runs the retry,
  backup and fall-back rules and the "Automatic (fastest)" ping, and a persisted queue that
  reports VPN events. A Supabase Realtime subscription to the member's own `member_playlist`
  rows, and a restart of the playing channel after a VPN or proxy change. Wiring in
  `kevbox/KevBoxCoordinator.kt` and `kevbox/di/KevBoxModule.kt`. Reading the new columns and the
  `vpn_server` configs in `supabase/SupabaseApi.kt`, `supabase/KevBoxModels.kt` and
  `session/KevBoxAuth.kt`. Manifest additions for the VPN service, which runs in the app's main
  process, and native-library packaging in `app/build.gradle.kts`. The release itself is a
  non-goal (see Non-goals).
- **kevbox, database (branch base `main`)**: migration `supabase/migrations/0023_live_vpn.sql`:
  VPN columns on `member_playlist`, a `vpn_server` table holding the Premiumize config files, a
  `live_vpn_event` table, VPN status columns on `live_device`, the functions
  `record_live_vpn_events_as` plus its JWT wrapper `record_live_vpn_events`, and
  `member_playlist` added to the `supabase_realtime` publication. Functions copied byte for byte
  into `packages/core/test/schema.sql`.
- **kevbox, VPN servers page**: a new page at `/vpn-servers` (`src/app/(dashboard)/vpn-servers/`),
  linked from the header menu (`src/components/header-content.tsx`). It fetches the 18 Premiumize
  `.ovpn` files from Premiumize's public addresses and lets Kevin set each server's backup.
- **kevbox, customer window**: a VPN switch and server dropdown (18 servers plus "Automatic
  (fastest)") in the KevBox Live box (`src/components/kevbox-live-section.tsx`), under the VPS
  proxy switch, and a VPN status line per TV. Server actions in `src/app/live-actions.ts`
  (including `setKevboxLiveProxy` and `sendToKevboxLive`); writes in `src/server/admin/`.
- **kevbox, keeping logins current**: the existing 15-minute refresh job
  (`deploy/kevbox-vps-proxy-refresh.*`, `scripts/refresh-vps-proxy-access.ts`) and the debrid
  actions in `src/app/actions.ts` (`debridAssign`, `forceDebridAssign`, `debridUnassign`,
  `updateDebridAccount`, `extendDebridExpiry`, `deleteDebridAccount`) also rewrite the VPN login,
  or turn the VPN off when the login is no longer complete.
- **kevbox, Telegram**: the existing 5-minute watch check (`src/app/api/cron/watch-check/route.ts`)
  also sends the VPN messages for TV events, through `src/lib/telegram.ts`. The "VPN turned off"
  message goes out from the step that turns the VPN off (AC-17).

## How it works

### Terms

- **Customer, TV, client number**: as in the 0.4 spec (`2026-10-15-0.4-watch-status-design.md`,
  Terms). A VPN setting belongs to one `member_playlist` row, so to one client number, the same
  way the proxy does.
- **Debrid login**: the customer's debrid account, found through Sheet1's `debrid_username`.
  The Debrid sheet gives its `customer_id` (the VPN username), `api_token` (the VPN password) and
  `expiry_date`. As in `groupDebridAccounts` (`src/lib/excel.ts`), the Debrid sheet's `api_token`
  and `expiry_date` win over Sheet1's.
- **Main server / backup server**: the server Kevin picks in the dropdown, and the one the
  VPN servers page names as its backup. With "Automatic (fastest)", the TV picks both at the
  start of each cycle (AC-41).
- **Server label**: the server's `label` from `vpn_server` ("Germany, Frankfurt"), or its host
  when it has no row.
- **Cycle**: one run of the retry rules, from the first try on the main server to either a
  working connection or a fall-back.
- **Fall-back**: the app gives up on the VPN for 3 hours and uses the TV's normal internet.

### The flow

1. Kevin turns the VPN on for a customer in kevbox.dev and picks a server. kevbox.dev writes
   the server (or `auto`), its backup and the debrid login into that customer's
   `member_playlist` row, and turns that customer's proxy off.
2. The TV hears of the change within seconds through its Realtime subscription (AC-39). The
   first time, Android shows its "allow VPN" prompt once.
3. The app connects. Only KevBox Live's own traffic goes through the tunnel.
4. If the connection fails or drops, the retry rules run (AC-30). If nothing works, the app
   falls back to normal internet with no message on screen and reports it. kevbox.dev pings
   Kevin on Telegram.
5. 3 hours later, if the switch is still on, the app starts a new cycle.

### Telegram messages

Fall-back, after the main server and its backup both failed:

> 🟠 KevBox Live: Kevin (client 12) VPN fell back to normal internet at 21:05. Germany, Frankfurt and Netherlands, Amsterdam both failed. Next try 00:05. https://kevbox.dev/customers?client=12&tab=live

Fall-back with no backup tried:

> 🟠 KevBox Live: Kevin (client 12) VPN fell back to normal internet at 21:05. Germany, Frankfurt failed. Next try 00:05. https://kevbox.dev/customers?client=12&tab=live

3 or more customers falling back within 15 minutes, one customer label per line below:

> 🟠 KevBox Live: 5 customers' VPN fell back since 21:00. Most likely a Premiumize problem.
> Kevin (client 12)
> Rami (client 31)
> …

Back on after a fall-back:

> 🟢 KevBox Live: Kevin (client 12) VPN connected again, Germany, Frankfurt, 00:06.

Prompt refused:

> 🟠 KevBox Live: Kevin (client 12) didn't allow the VPN on TCL 65C755 · 3f2a. Someone needs to press OK on the TV. https://kevbox.dev/customers?client=12&tab=live

Another VPN app running:

> 🟠 KevBox Live: Kevin (client 12) has another VPN app running on TCL 65C755 · 3f2a, so KevBox's VPN can't start. https://kevbox.dev/customers?client=12&tab=live

Login no longer complete:

> 🟠 KevBox Live: VPN turned off for Kevin (client 12): Customer ID missing. https://kevbox.dev/customers?client=12&tab=live

Rules for every VPN message:

- The customer label and the link follow the 0.4 rules: the link opens the KevBox Live tab of the
  first client number, or the member link when there is none.
- The TV is named as in the 0.4 messages: its model and the last 4 characters of its device id,
  or "TV" when the model is unknown.
- Times are Beirut time as "HH:mm", with "dd/MM " in front when not today (the 0.4 format),
  taken from the event's `received_at`. "Next try" is the fall-back time plus 3 hours.
- "Connected again" always goes out silent. Every other VPN message has sound, except between
  00:00 and 07:59 Beirut, when it goes out silent.

## Acceptance criteria

### kevbox: database (0023_live_vpn.sql)

- **AC-1** `member_playlist` gains `vpn_enabled boolean not null default false`, `vpn_server
  text`, `vpn_backup_server text`, `vpn_username text`, `vpn_password text`, `vpn_changed_at
  timestamptz` (when kevbox.dev last switched the VPN on or changed its server; AC-33). `vpn_server` holds
  a host from `vpn_server` or the word `auto` (AC-41); with `auto`, `vpn_backup_server` is null.
  A check rejects a row with `vpn_enabled` true and a blank `vpn_server`, `vpn_username` or
  `vpn_password`. A second check rejects a row with both `vpn_enabled` and `proxy_enabled` true.
  Members read the new columns of their own rows under the existing lock rule (0013), and
  nothing while locked.
- **AC-2** A table `vpn_server`: `host text primary key` (for example `vpn-de.premiumize.me`),
  `label text not null`, `config text not null` (the `.ovpn` file), `backup_host text`
  referencing `vpn_server(host)`, `sort_order int not null`, `updated_at timestamptz`.
  `authenticated` may select; `kevbox_admin` may select, insert, update, delete.
- **AC-3** A table `live_vpn_event` (user_id, device_id, client_number, kind, server, at,
  received_at, telegram_sent_at). `kind` is one of `connected`, `fell_back`,
  `permission_denied`, `other_vpn_active`. For `fell_back`, `server` holds the hosts tried, in
  order, joined by a comma. Grants follow `live_error` (0015): members select their own rows,
  writes go through AC-4's functions only, and `kevbox_admin` may select and set
  `telegram_sent_at`. `live_device` gains `last_vpn_state`, `last_vpn_server`, `last_vpn_at`,
  set from the last entry of each accepted batch (the TV sends entries oldest first);
  `last_vpn_at` is that entry's `received_at`.
- **AC-4** `record_live_vpn_events(p_device_id, p_app_version, p_entries jsonb)` is a JWT
  wrapper over the security definer `record_live_vpn_events_as`, following
  `record_live_errors`. It reads only the first 50 entries of a call. It skips an entry with an
  unknown `kind` or a `server` longer than 100 characters and stores the rest. It stores
  `client_number` as null when the caller has no `member_playlist` row with that client number.
- **AC-38** The migration adds `member_playlist` to the `supabase_realtime` publication, so a
  signed-in member receives changes to their own rows (the 0013 read rule applies) and nobody
  else's.
- **AC-5** The new functions are copied byte for byte into `packages/core/test/schema.sql`, and
  vitest proves AC-1 to AC-4 on the test database.

### kevbox: VPN servers page

- **AC-6** A "VPN servers" page in the header menu has a "Fetch from Premiumize" button that
  downloads the 18 OpenVPN files from
  `https://www.premiumize.me/static/tutorials/vpn/openvpn/vpn-<code>.premiumize.me.ovpn` and
  adds or replaces one `vpn_server` row per file, keyed by the host in its first `remote` line.
  The page fetches by itself the first time it opens with no rows. A file that fails to
  download, or has no `remote` line, leaves that server's existing row as it was, and the page
  lists it as "Couldn't fetch <host>". The page strips any inline `<auth-user-pass>` block
  before saving. There is no upload.
- **AC-7** Each row's label comes from a built-in map of the 18 Premiumize hosts on
  premiumize.me/vpn/locations ("Germany, Frankfurt" for `vpn-de.premiumize.me`, and so on for
  de, ch, nl, gb, fr, be, at, es, fi, it, gr, cz, pl, us, ca, sg, au, jp); a host not in the map
  shows the host itself. `sort_order` follows that page's order.
- **AC-8** A row with no backup gets a neighbour from a built-in map (de→nl, nl→de, ch→de,
  at→de, cz→de, pl→de, be→nl, gb→nl, fr→gb, es→fr, it→de, gr→de, fi→de, us→ca, ca→us, sg→jp,
  au→sg, jp→sg), only when that neighbour has a row after the fetch. A re-fetch keeps a backup
  already set. Kevin can change each row's backup with a dropdown; saving it also rewrites
  `vpn_backup_server` on every `member_playlist` row whose `vpn_server` is that host.

### kevbox: customer window

- **AC-9** The KevBox Live box shows a "Premiumize VPN" switch and a server dropdown listing
  "Automatic (fastest)" first, then `vpn_server` rows by `sort_order`. With the switch off, the
  dropdown starts on "Automatic (fastest)"; with it on, the dropdown shows the saved server.
  Every customer starts with the switch off.
- **AC-10** The switch is disabled, with the reason shown beside it, when the customer is not on
  KevBox Live ("Not on KevBox Live"), has no debrid account ("No debrid account"), has a blank
  `customer_id` ("Customer ID missing") or a blank API key ("API key missing"), has a debrid
  expiry date before today in Beirut ("Debrid account expired on <dd/MM/yyyy>"), or when no
  servers have been fetched ("No VPN servers fetched"). A blank expiry date does not disable it.
- **AC-11** Turning it on writes to the customer's `member_playlist` row: `vpn_enabled` true,
  `vpn_server` (the chosen host, or `auto`), `vpn_backup_server` (that host's `backup_host`, or
  null with `auto`), `vpn_username` (the Debrid sheet's `customer_id`), `vpn_password` (its
  `api_token`) and `vpn_changed_at` (now). In the same action it turns that customer's VPS proxy off, both on the
  "VPS Proxy" sheet and in `member_playlist`. It writes a `live.vpn` audit row.
- **AC-12** Turning the VPS proxy on for a customer whose VPN is on turns the VPN off in the
  same action, clearing the fields as AC-13 does. The same happens when "Send to KevBox Live"
  writes a proxy that is on. Send and Edit otherwise leave the `vpn_*` fields as they are.
- **AC-13** Turning the VPN off sets `vpn_enabled` false and `vpn_server`, `vpn_backup_server`,
  `vpn_username`, `vpn_password` and `vpn_changed_at` null.
- **AC-14** Changing the dropdown while the switch is on rewrites `vpn_server`,
  `vpn_backup_server` and `vpn_changed_at` straight away, by the rules of AC-11.
- **AC-15** While the customer's VPN switch is on, under each TV the box shows its VPN status
  from `live_device`: "VPN connected · Germany, Frankfurt · 20:01" (the label of the server the
  TV reported, so with `auto` the server it actually used), "VPN fell back to normal internet at
  21:05 · next try 00:05", "Waiting for someone to press OK on the TV", or "Another VPN app is
  running". It shows nothing for a TV that has never reported a VPN event, and nothing at all
  while the switch is off.

### kevbox: keeping logins current

- **AC-16** Each debrid action in `src/app/actions.ts` (`debridAssign`, `forceDebridAssign`,
  `debridUnassign`, `updateDebridAccount`, `extendDebridExpiry`, `deleteDebridAccount`), and
  every run of the 15-minute refresh job, rewrite `vpn_username` and `vpn_password` on every
  `member_playlist` row whose VPN is on, from the current Debrid sheet.
- **AC-17** If a row's debrid login has become incomplete or expired, the same step turns that
  row's VPN off (as AC-13) and sends one "VPN turned off" message through `src/lib/telegram.ts`
  naming the reason, with the texts of AC-10: "No debrid account", "Customer ID missing", "API
  key missing" or "Debrid account expired on <dd/MM/yyyy>". The whole expiry day still counts as
  valid. A failed send is not retried; the disabled switch shows the reason (AC-10).

### kevbox: Telegram

- **AC-18** Each watch-check run sends one message for each `fell_back` event not yet sent, in
  the format under Telegram messages, naming the server labels from the event's `server`. It
  sets `telegram_sent_at` only after Telegram accepts a message, so a failed send is retried on
  the next run; this holds for every VPN message from the watch check. When 3 or more customers'
  unsent `fell_back` events have a `received_at` within 15 minutes of the earliest one, they go
  out as one message instead, following the 0.4 grouping rule: "🟠 KevBox Live: 5 customers' VPN
  fell back since 21:00. Most likely a Premiumize problem.", with each customer's label on its
  own line below.
- **AC-19** `permission_denied` and `other_vpn_active` send at most one message per TV per kind
  per Beirut day, in the formats under Telegram messages. Later events of the same TV and kind
  that day get `telegram_sent_at` set with no message.
- **AC-20** A `connected` event whose previous VPN event on the same TV is a `fell_back` sends
  the "connected again" message, always silent. Other `connected` events send nothing.
- **AC-21** Between 00:00 and 07:59 Beirut every VPN message goes out silent. Customers the 0.4
  rules silence (all client numbers cancelled or expired) get no VPN message.

### App: engine and settings

- **AC-22** The app builds with the OpenVPN for Android engine in both flavours (`standard`:
  arm64-v8a and armeabi-v7a; `x86_64`). The `standard` release APK grows by at most 15 MB over
  61,776,028 bytes.
- **AC-23** The playlist read asks for the six `vpn_*` columns along with the proxy columns. If
  the server answers 400 with code `42703`, it asks again without the `vpn_*` columns and
  treats the VPN as off; if that also answers 400 with `42703`, the existing fallback without
  the proxy columns applies. A row reads as VPN on only when `vpn_enabled` is true and server,
  username and password are non-blank.
- **AC-24** When the chosen row's VPN is on, the app reads the `config` of its main and backup
  servers from `vpn_server` (all rows when `vpn_server` is `auto`). A missing config counts as a
  failed try on that server.
- **AC-41** With `vpn_server` set to `auto`, at the start of each cycle (never mid-cycle) the
  app pings every server in `vpn_server` 3 times, outside the tunnel, all servers at once, giving
  up on a server after 2 seconds. It uses the lowest average as the main server and the second
  lowest as the backup, and keeps them until the next cycle. A server that never answers is
  skipped. If none answers, it uses the first two by `sort_order`. The `connected` and
  `fell_back` events name the servers it actually used.
- **AC-25** `ManagedVpn` follows `ManagedProxy`. It uses the chosen playlist's VPN, or the only
  playlist's when none is chosen. It applies after every successful playlist check and on a
  playlist pick, disconnects and clears on sign-out, and changes nothing after a failed check.
  If a row has both VPN and proxy on, the VPN wins and the app applies the proxy as off.
- **AC-39** While signed in, the app keeps a Supabase Realtime subscription to its own
  `member_playlist` rows. Any change event runs the playlist check within 5 seconds, so a VPN
  switch, a server change, a proxy switch or a sent playlist reaches the TV without a restart
  or the 15-minute wait. A dropped subscription reconnects with a growing delay (5 s doubling to
  5 minutes). The 15-minute check and the check on return to the front stay as the safety net.
- **AC-40** When a VPN or proxy change takes effect while a channel plays (switched on, off,
  server change, or a silent fall-back), the app restarts that channel by itself within 3
  seconds of the new connection being ready. The customer sees a pause and nothing else.
  Switching the proxy on or off in kevbox.dev applies on the TV without an app restart.

### App: connecting

- **AC-26** When a VPN is wanted and Android's VPN permission is missing, the app shows
  Android's prompt straight away if the app is in front, even over a playing channel. If the
  app is in the background, it shows the prompt the moment the app returns to the front. It
  asks at most once per app start. OK connects. Cancel or Back reports `permission_denied`. The
  app does not ask again until the next app start or until kevbox.dev changes that row's VPN
  settings.
- **AC-27** The tunnel carries only KevBox Live's own traffic: the app's package is the only
  allowed app (`VpnService.Builder.addAllowedApplication`). Every other app on the TV keeps its
  normal connection, whether the tunnel is up, connecting or failing. The app's own calls to the
  KevBox Supabase go through the tunnel while it is up.
- **AC-28** The app connects with `vpn_username` as the username, `vpn_password` as the
  password and the server's `config`. A connection that is up within 30 seconds counts as a
  success and reports `connected` with the server's host; one that is not up by then counts as
  a failed connect (AC-30).
- **AC-29** If another app's VPN is active on the TV, the app does not start its own, reports
  `other_vpn_active`, and checks again at the next playlist check. This holds inside a cycle too:
  another VPN found before any try, or after a drop, ends the cycle with `other_vpn_active` and no
  `fell_back`.

### App: retry and fall-back

- **AC-30** A failed connect, or a drop that lasts more than 30 seconds, counts as one failure.
  After a failure the app waits 60 seconds and tries the same server again. After 3 failures on
  the main server it tries the backup server, with the same 3 tries 60 seconds apart. A cycle
  changes server at most once. With no backup set, the cycle ends after the main server's 3rd
  failure. These rules override the engine's own reconnecting: Premiumize's config files carry
  `resolv-retry infinite`, which would otherwise retry forever.
- **AC-31** When a cycle ends without a connection, the app disconnects the VPN and carries on
  over normal internet, with no message on screen. It reports `fell_back` naming the servers
  tried (AC-3), and stores the fall-back time.
- **AC-32** 3 hours after the stored fall-back time, if the latest playlist check still says VPN
  on, the app starts a new cycle on the main server. If the switch is off by then, nothing
  happens. Restarting the app inside the 3 hours does not start a cycle early.
- **AC-33** Switching the VPN off in kevbox.dev disconnects the TV within 10 seconds (AC-39).
  Changing the server reconnects to the new one the same way and clears any stored fall-back
  time. Switching it off and on again clears a stored fall-back time and starts a new cycle at
  once, so Kevin can force a retry. The TV tells a switch-on or server change by `vpn_changed_at`,
  so an off then on it never read in between (a quick toggle, a reconnecting subscription, a
  sleeping TV) still starts a new cycle.
- **AC-34** Apart from Android's one-time prompt, the VPN shows nothing on screen: no toast,
  dialog or banner. Its notification uses the lowest importance Android allows for the service.
- **AC-37** 10 minutes after KevBox Live leaves the screen, the app disconnects the VPN. When the
  app returns to the front it reconnects to the server it last used, unless a fall-back is in
  force. This disconnect and reconnect is not a failure, starts no new cycle, and leaves a
  stored fall-back time as it is. When the app process starts without coming to the front (at
  boot, or for a background job), the VPN does not connect until KevBox Live is on screen, and no
  new cycle starts while it is off screen.

### App: reporting

- **AC-35** VPN events go into a persisted queue, like `kevbox/errors/ErrorQueue.kt`, and are
  sent with `record_live_vpn_events`, oldest first. Events from a broken tunnel are sent after
  the fall-back, over normal internet. A failed send keeps the events for the next try.
- **AC-36** Unit tests with a fake clock and a fake engine cover AC-25, AC-26, AC-28 to AC-35,
  AC-37 and AC-41. Parsing tests cover AC-23. During the live test with Kevin present
  (Decisions), a check on the `kevbox_tv` emulator proves AC-27: with the tunnel up, an
  address-echo request from KevBox Live returns a Premiumize address while the same request
  from `adb shell` returns the emulator's normal address.

## Non-goals

- **Sending other apps through the VPN.** The tunnel exists for KevBox Live; YouTube or Netflix
  on the same TV stay on normal internet.
- **A VPN setting the customer can see or change on the TV.** kevbox.dev is the only place it
  changes, as with the proxy.
- **SoftEther, WireGuard or any provider but Premiumize.** Premiumize offers OpenVPN and
  SoftEther; only OpenVPN has a usable Android engine.
- **Telling the customer about a fall-back.** Kevin chose a silent fall-back.
- **Counting how many VPN connections one Premiumize account allows.** Premiumize's limit is
  unknown (its VPN pages need a login); the first test on Kevin's account finds out.
- **Releasing.** Migration 0023 on the live KevBox Supabase (after a backup), the kevbox.dev
  deploy to persovps and the app release via `release.sh` each wait for Kevin's approval at the
  time.

## Decisions

- **Engine built into the app (approach A)**, over driving a separately installed OpenVPN app
  (B: a second app and two prompts per TV, whole-TV routing) and over encrypting the existing
  Squid proxy (C: all video on persovps, no Premiumize servers). Kevin wants it built in and
  silent.
- **Licence mismatch accepted.** The engine is GPL version 2 only; the app is GPL version 3.
  Kevin chose A knowing this.
- **One switch per `member_playlist` row**, matching the proxy, over one switch per login.
- **Proxy and VPN are either/or**, over allowing both: together they add nothing, and the
  database check stops it.
- **Conservative retry: 3 tries per server, 60 seconds apart, at most one server change per
  cycle**, over automatic hopping. Premiumize's terms ban "use the Service with several IPs in a
  short time period (e.g., but not limited to, frequent VPN server changes …)", and a flagged
  account cuts off every customer sharing it.
- **Silent fall-back to normal internet with a Telegram ping and a retry after 3 hours**, over
  stopping playback. Kevin's call.
- **Config files live in the database, fetched by kevbox.dev from Premiumize's public
  addresses** (grill round 3), over shipping them inside the app (a changed Premiumize server
  would need an app release) and over Kevin uploading them (18 manual downloads).
- **Telegram goes out from the existing watch check**, over a new timer: one 5-minute job already
  sends, dedupes and retries.
- **First rollout on Kevin's own account (kevin.chiha@gmail.com) only**, before any customer.
- Size: kept as one phase at the user's word (41 criteria)
- **VPN login is Customer ID as username, API key as password** (grill round 1). Premiumize's
  Android TV guide for OpenVPN for Android says so in its step 5.
- **One backup per server, set on the VPN servers page** (grill round 1), over a second dropdown
  per customer: less to set per customer. Most default neighbours (AC-8) are servers
  Premiumize's locations page marks as high bandwidth.
- **An expired debrid account blocks the switch, and expiry turns a running VPN off with one
  message** (grill round 1), over allowing it with a warning: an expired account would only fail,
  fall back and ping every 3 hours.
- **Android's prompt appears straight away, even over a playing channel** (grill round 1), over
  waiting for the next time the app comes to the front: Kevin wants the switch to act at once.
- **The build may use the `kevbox_tv` emulator for its tests without asking** (grill round 1).
  Never the real TCL TV. Kevin's KevBox login for tests is in
  `.git/phase-args-0.6-premiumize-vpn/test-login.env`, outside every commit; never copy it into
  a tracked file, a log or a report. A live connection to Premiumize with Kevin's debrid login
  happens after the build, with Kevin present, before any release.
- **The VPN drops 10 minutes after the app leaves the screen and comes back on return** (grill
  round 1), over staying up: a shared Premiumize account may limit how many connections run at
  once.
- **Only KevBox Live's traffic goes through the VPN** (grill round 1, Kevin's emphasis), proven on
  the emulator (AC-36).
- **3 or more fall-backs within 15 minutes go out as one Telegram message** (grill round 1),
  matching the "can't watch" alerts.
- **Switch changes reach the TV at once through a Supabase Realtime subscription, for the VPN

  engine declares its services with `android:process=":openvpn"`; the app's manifest removes that
  attribute from every merged engine component, so there is one process, one controller, one event
  queue and one fall-back store, and the engine's in-process status listeners report straight to
  the controller. Rejected: keeping the `:openvpn` process, with `OwnTVApp.onCreate` returning early
  there and the controller talking to the engine through its cross-process status service. That
  needs an `OwnTVApp` edit, a cross-process status protocol and a clean-up for a tunnel that
  outlives the main process, and a missed guard starts a second controller against the same
  engine. In one process a killed app takes its tunnel with it, which AC-37 wants anyway.

  packed, which makes every connect fail. The libraries then ship compressed, so the APK gets
  smaller and the installed app larger; AC-22's limit is measured on the APK after this change.
