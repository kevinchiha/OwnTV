**Refuter 2** (stands, critical). The finding holds up, and the problem would show up when the app runs, not just in theory. The opposite choice is no fix either. If the builder strips the process attribute, IcsVpnEngine's status mapping depends on the engine's in-process status listeners, and the plan never says which way to go.
