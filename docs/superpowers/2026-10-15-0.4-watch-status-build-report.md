### Part 1: findings

Important
- I-1. OwnTV `usage/PlayWatcher.kt:64-92` (PlayRun proves once per run) + `PlayNotes.kt:102` (onFailureRecorded only bumps a counter), with kevbox `packages/core/src/liveWatch.ts:194` (failures counted when last_at > last_play_at). A failure recorded while a proven run keeps playing never leads to a later note. Example: the startup channel proves at 30 s, then a channel-list sync fails (blocking) and the customer watches the same channel for an hour: a false "can't watch" 15 minutes later, red until they change channel. Goal + AC-4. Fix: on a recorded failure during a live run, restart that run's 30-second count.
- I-2. OwnTV `PlayWatcher.kt:158`: full-screen "playing" is Core's `OwnTVPlayer.isPlaying`, true from the start of an mpv load and through the silent retry ladder and offline waits (only `_buffering` goes true). 30 s of a stream that never shows a frame proves a run, the note clears the incident and Kevin gets a false "watching again". AC-1, AC-17. Fix: count the player only while `isPlaying && !buffering` (plus a CoreContractTest pin).
- I-3. kevbox `0016_live_watch.sql:226-241` (server merge ignores plays) + `liveWatch.ts:103-114, :188-197`. A row merged across a good play counts its pre-play repeats and waits from its old `first_received_at`: one failure after a play can open an incident (AC-15), and a just-cleared incident can reopen at once with the old "since" (AC-18). Option A: the merge never joins a row whose `last_at <= last_play_at`. Option B: the check counts such a row as 1 and waits from `max(first_received_at, last_play_received_at)`.
- I-4. kevbox `src/app/api/cron/watch-check/route.ts:43-51` + `deploy/README.md:142`. The "Telegram not set" warning and `telegram:"unconfigured"` only appear when a message is due; with nothing to send the body says "ok", and the README's first-run check passes a deploy that forgot the env values. AC-27. Fix: test both values once at the top of the route.
- I-5 (Out of reach item 1, still open). kevbox `0015_live_error.sql:320` "To restore: re-run this whole file" undoes the server half of AC-32 after 0016. Needs one comment line: re-run 0016 afterwards.

Minor (new)
- m-1. OwnTV `ErrorQueue.kt:70` lets a null latest flag wipe a known one; 0016:240 keeps it (coalesce); UPSTREAM-SYNC.md:444 claims they follow the same rule.
- m-2. OwnTV play notes read the proxy setting at 30 s, but mpv sets http-proxy once per load: a long mpv stream opened before the switch can report `p_proxy` true. Affects only the proxy words.
- m-3. OwnTV PlayWatcherTest covers only pure PlayRun/playLookOf; nothing drives watch()/look().
