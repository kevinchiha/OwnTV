and Task 6's shared pieces, so kevbox builds in two steps. Every worktree is cut from its repo's
`phase/0.5-live-usage`, not from the base.

Runs A and B run at the same time; each repo has its own gate, so they are separate
`phase-build-lanes` runs. Longest path: 3 → 4 → 5 → 6 → (10 → 11 → 12) → 13, eight tasks of
thirteen, so about 1.6 times as fast as one at a time, not more.

Tasks 3 to 6 are a chain (each consumes the one before). In step 2, lane-3 (7) writes the cron
routes and deploy units, lane-4 (8 → 9) the Usage tab and the customer window (Task 9 mounts
Task 8's tab), lane-5 (10 → 11 → 12) the KevBox Live page (Task 12 mounts 10 and 11). No file is
written by two lanes.
